Geometrical Constructions

1 lesson · 15 min
01 Chapter 1: Geometrical Constructions Build angle bisectors, perpendicular bisectors, incentre and circumcentre with a ruler and compass. Interactive Class 7 Self paced

What your child will learn

  • Draw an angle bisector and a perpendicular bisector.
  • Find the incentre and circumcentre of a triangle.
  • Construct triangles from SSS, SAS, ASA and RHS information.
  • Recognise congruent segments, angles, circles and cuboids.
